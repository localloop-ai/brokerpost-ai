Frequently asked questions

1Who is BrokerPost built for?⌄

BrokerPost is built for independent Canadian insurance brokerages that want consistent marketing but do not want to plan and write everything internally or manage another do-it-yourself platform.

+Is this just AI-generated content?⌄

No. AI supports production, but BrokerPost supplies the structured agency profile, monthly direction, province-conscious rules, content coordination, quality review, and organized delivery.

+Do you provide insurance advice?⌄

No. BrokerPost provides general marketing content and planning services. We do not provide coverage, policy, claims, legal, or personalized insurance advice.

+Is the content compliance-approved?⌄

No. Content is designed to be cautious and ready for brokerage review, but the brokerage remains responsible for confirming all agency-specific, insurer-specific, licensing, advertising, and regulatory requirements before publication.

+Does BrokerPost publish the content?⌄

Not in the standard package. Your team receives ready-to-review content and a clear publishing calendar. Publishing and account management are not included at launch.

+Do you guarantee leads or sales?⌄

No. BrokerPost provides a consistent marketing process and prepared content. Results also depend on distribution, audience, offer, reputation, follow-up, and market conditions.

+What does BrokerPost Premium cost?⌄

Standard pricing begins at $750 CAD per month, plus a one-time onboarding fee. Custom requirements, extra brands, or distinct additional locations may require separate pricing.

+Why is there an onboarding fee?⌄

Onboarding covers the review of your brokerage, services, audience, voice, restrictions, priorities, website, and initial Agency Marketing Blueprint that guides future packages.

+Is there a minimum commitment?⌄

The initial service term is three months. This provides enough time to complete onboarding, deliver multiple marketing cycles, and determine whether the system is a strong fit.

+Can we request changes?⌄

BrokerPost Premium includes one consolidated revision round for reasonable tone changes, factual corrections, and minor content adjustments. Additional rounds or new campaign directions are priced separately.
